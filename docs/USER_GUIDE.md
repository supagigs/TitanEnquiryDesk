# Titan Enquiry Desk — user guide

Open **/titan-desk** on the site. A short tour runs on your first visit; restart it from **Take the tour** in the sidebar.

## Today
- **Needs a person**: enquiries the desk escalated (price, delivery date, drawing, complaint, hot lead). Open the chat to answer.
- **Being followed up**: leads still on automatic follow-up, how much of the requirement is captured and what is missing.
- Goals, sources and product lines for the week.

## Inbox
- Filter chats: *Needs human*, *Following up*, *All*. Search by name, company or number.
- Reply on **WhatsApp** or **Email**. Anything you send pauses automatic follow-ups for that lead.
- WhatsApp rule: within 24 hours of the customer's last message you can write freely; after that choose an approved template.
- **Take over** makes you the owner. **Pause/Resume follow-ups** controls automation for the lead. **Mark handled** clears an escalation.
- **Requirement sheet**: choose the product line and fill the details as you learn them; the percentage updates automatically.

## Pipeline
Drag a card to another stage (or use the stage menu). Changes go to Zoho (simulated until FlowForge provides live sync).

## Calendar
Drag a lead from **Calls to schedule** onto a time. A 30-minute call is booked, you are assigned, and follow-ups pause.

## Approvals
While supervised mode is on (5 weeks by default), automatic follow-ups wait here. Edit the text, then **Approve & send** or **Reject** with a reason.

## Settings
Set your own follow-up intervals (hours, comma separated), maximum follow-ups and quiet hours.
